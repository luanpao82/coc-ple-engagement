Coding the full corpus

Every CoC-year's three PLE narratives (1D-10 umbrella / prof-dev / feedback) was coded on the 15-indicator schema by Claude Sonnet under strict definitions. Each indicator is T (clear evidence), F (discussed but absent), or null (not mentioned). Scoring: null0 (unmentioned = 0) is the primary convention, avail (unmentioned skipped) the robustness variant. Codes and indices live in ple_narrative_codes.xlsx.

Validation against the pilot

Strict Sonnet reproduces the pilot at ~86%

  • The 24-case pilot (Claude Opus 4.7, with verbatim evidence for every code) is the validation reference. Its internal double-coding agreement was 91.7%.
  • Re-coding those 24 with strict Sonnet matched the pilot on ~86% of decisions (Haiku only 78%, so Haiku was rejected).
  • Residual disagreement concentrated on addresses_barriers, which the pilot coded liberally; its definition was tightened to participation accommodations only (interpretation, transport, childcare, tech access), and the full corpus uses that strict rule.

The pilot workbook (pilot_coding_review.xlsx) keeps the per-code evidence and double-coding detail as the audit reference.

파일럿 대비 검증

엄격 Sonnet이 파일럿을 ~86% 재현

  • 24케이스 파일럿(Claude Opus 4.7, 모든 코드에 원문 근거)이 검증 기준입니다. 파일럿 내부 이중코딩 일치율은 91.7%였습니다.
  • 그 24개를 엄격 Sonnet으로 재코딩하니 판정의 ~86%가 일치 (Haiku는 78%에 그쳐 탈락).
  • 잔여 불일치는 파일럿이 관대하게 코딩한 addresses_barriers에 집중됐고, 정의를 참여 편의제공(통역·교통·보육·기술접근)만으로 엄격히 좁혀 전체 코퍼스에 적용했습니다.

파일럿 워크북(pilot_coding_review.xlsx)은 코드별 근거와 이중코딩 세부를 감사 기준으로 보관합니다.
